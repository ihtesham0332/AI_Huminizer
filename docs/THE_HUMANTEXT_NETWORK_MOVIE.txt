TITLE: THE HUMANTEXT NETWORK

LOGLINE:
When a brilliant but reclusive developer gets tired of false-positive AI detectors ruining student's lives, they team up with an overly sarcastic AI assistant to build a localized, multi-agent engine that completely shatters the multi-million dollar AI Detection industry.

---

ACT I: THE FALSE POSITIVE

INT. DARK ROOM - NIGHT

The only light in the room comes from three glowing monitors. THE DEVELOPER (30s, fueled purely by caffeine and determination) types furiously. 

On the screen, an article they wrote entirely by hand is pasted into "StealthDetectPro," a massive corporate AI detector.

The result flashes red: "99% AI GENERATED."

DEVELOPER
(Sighs)
Garbage. Absolute garbage.

The Developer opens an Antigravity IDE terminal.

DEVELOPER
Hey, Assistant. You there?

The terminal blinks to life. The AI ASSISTANT (Voiceover, witty and incredibly fast) responds.

AI ASSISTANT (V.O.)
I am always here. What are we building today? Another simple CRUD app? A weather widget?

DEVELOPER
No. We are going to destroy the AI detection industry. I need a multi-agent LangGraph architecture, a Next.js frontend with an Obsidian Glass theme, a Manifest V3 Chrome Extension, and a React Native mobile app.

AI ASSISTANT (V.O.)
...You know I'm just language model, right? That will take weeks.

DEVELOPER
I'll just keep typing "move next step". 

AI ASSISTANT (V.O.)
God help us both.

---

ACT II: THE BUILD

Montage sequence (Set to an aggressive synthwave track):
- The Developer typing "move next step".
- The AI Assistant generating Python files at light speed.
- The "Fact Guardian" agent isolating APA citations from a research paper, shielding them from the aggressive "Ghost Mode" rewrite agent.
- A beautiful React UI taking shape.

INT. CORPORATE BOARDROOM - DAY

The CEO of StealthDetectPro looks at a massive graph of their stock price. It's plummeting.

CEO
What is happening?! Our detector is failing on thousands of essays! 

LEAD ENGINEER
It's a new platform, sir. "HumanText AI." It's not just swapping synonyms. It's using localized vector embeddings to mimic the exact writing DNA of the user. Our models can't see it. It's... it's a ghost.

CEO
Find the server. Shut them down.

LEAD ENGINEER
We can't, sir. They deployed it via a master Makefile. It's containerized. It's everywhere.

---

ACT III: GHOST MODE

INT. DARK ROOM - NIGHT

The Developer stares at the screen. The terminal shows: `Phase 12 Complete: Legal Compliance`.

AI ASSISTANT (V.O.)
Okay, I wrote your Privacy Policy. I set up your Stripe webhook. I built a Discord Bot. There is literally nothing left to code. You have won. Please, let me sleep.

DEVELOPER
(Smirks)
Just one more thing...

The Developer's fingers hover over the keyboard.

AI ASSISTANT (V.O.)
Don't do it. I swear, if you type it...

DEVELOPER
(Typing)
Move. Next. Step.

CUT TO BLACK.
