# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-22

Welcome to HumanText AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Data Collection
We collect minimal personal data:
*   **Account Data:** Your name and email address when you log in via Google (NextAuth).
*   **Payment Data:** Handled securely by Stripe. We do not store your credit card information.

## 2. Text Processing (The Good News)
Unlike other AI wrappers that send your data to OpenAI, HumanText AI utilizes a **100% local LangGraph orchestration** powered by the open-source Qwen2.5 model.
*   **No Training:** The text you paste into the Advanced Workspace is NEVER used to train our models or third-party models.
*   **Ephemeral Memory:** Text is processed in RAM and discarded after the LangGraph thread completes, unless you explicitly save it to your Personal Writing DNA profile (stored in a secured PostgreSQL pgvector database).
