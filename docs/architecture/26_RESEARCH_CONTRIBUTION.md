# 26. RESEARCH CONTRIBUTION

The development of this platform will yield a novel architectural approach to AI writing that can be documented academically.

## Proposed Research Title
> **Cost-Aware Multi-Agent Personalized Writing Transformation with Semantic, Factual, Citation, Style, and Consistency Verification**

## Core Research Questions (RQs)
1.  **Selective Rewriting:** Can an AI system reduce inference costs by 60%+ by pre-scoring sentences for transformation "need" without negatively impacting the user-perceived quality of the final document?
2.  **Integrity Verification:** Does decoupling the text *generation* from fact/citation *verification* into separate LLM/Deterministic agents significantly reduce the rate of "meaning drift" and hallucinations compared to zero-shot humanization?
3.  **Personalized DNA:** Does extracting syntactical preferences (Writing DNA) from a user's corpus prior to generation result in a lower rate of manual post-editing by the user?

## Output
We will produce a whitepaper documenting the ablation studies (comparing our Multi-Agent Graph vs. a single-prompt ChatGPT baseline) to publish as marketing material and technical proof of our "Moat".
